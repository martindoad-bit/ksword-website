About Us

会社概要

凱業株式会社（英文名：KSWORD）は、大阪を拠点に、日本刀・刀剣類、鍔・刀装具、刃物類を取り扱う会社です。

一点ごとの来歴や状態を尊重し、確認と記録を丁寧に行うことを基本姿勢としています。日本の刀剣文化と職人の技に敬意を払い、安心してご相談いただける取引を積み重ねてまいります。

古物営業法をはじめとする関係法令を遵守し、適正な事業運営に努めています。

About Us

About KSWORD

KSWORD (Kaigyou Co., Ltd.) is an Osaka-based company handling Japanese swords, fittings, and cutlery.

We respect the provenance and condition of every item, with careful verification and recordkeeping at the heart of our work. Our aim is to provide dependable, considered service grounded in respect for Japanese sword culture and craftsmanship.

We conduct our business responsibly and in accordance with Japan's Secondhand Articles Dealer Act and other applicable laws.
